# Voyage Travel & Refund Policy
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Document ID:** VOY-POL-2024-CR

## 1. Introduction
At Voyage, we strive to make travel seamless. This policy outlines the terms regarding cancellations, refunds, and modifications for all services. By booking with us, you agree to these terms.

## 2. Flight Booking Tiers
Your refund eligibility depends strictly on the fare tier selected.

### 2.1 Saver Fares (Economy Light)
* **Refund Status:** Non-refundable.
* **Changes:** Not permitted.
* **Exceptions:** If the airline cancels the flight, a full refund will be issued.
* **Note:** Saver fares do not include checked baggage.

### 2.2 Standard Fares (Main Cabin)
* **Cancellation Window:**
    * **More than 48 hours before departure:** 100% refundable to original payment method.
    * **Within 48 hours of departure:** 50% cancellation fee applies. The remaining 50% is issued as Voyage Credits.
    * **Within 4 hours of departure:** Non-refundable.
* **Changes:** One date change is permitted per booking, subject to fare difference.

### 2.3 Premium Fares (Business/First)
* **Refund Status:** Fully refundable up to 2 hours before departure.
* **No-Show Policy:** If you fail to check in, the ticket value is converted to Voyage Credits valid for 1 year.

## 3. Legacy Policy (Pre-2024)
*Note: This section is for historical reference and applies only to bookings made before Jan 1, 2024.*
* **All Tiers:** Previously, all tickets were 100% refundable if cancelled within 24 hours of booking, regardless of departure time. This has been superseded by the 2024 tiers.
* **Work from Home:** Employees were previously required to be in the office 5 days a week. As of 2024, we are Remote-First.

## 4. Refund Processing Times
* **Voyage Credits:** Instant.
* **Credit/Debit Card:** 5-7 business days.
* **PayPal:** 24-48 hours.

## 5. Exceptional Circumstances
Voyage may waive fees for "Force Majeure" events (e.g., severe weather grounding flights, death of immediate family). Documentation is required within 14 days. Minor illnesses without hospitalization do not qualify.

## 6. Baggage Fees
* **Economy:** $50 per checked bag.
* **Business:** 2 bags included.
* **Special Items:** Surfboards and Skis incur a non-refundable $75 fee per leg.

## 7. Contact Support
* **Urgent:** Call +1-800-VOYAGE-NOW.
* **General:** Use the "My Trips" tab in the app.
* **Escalations:** Email refunds@voyage.com. Do not contact the CEO directly.
